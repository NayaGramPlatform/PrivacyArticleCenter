NayaGram Privacy Policy

📅 Last Updated: October 02, 2026 • 🛡️ Effective Date: October 02, 2026 • 🏛️ Operator: NayaGram Platform
Welcome to NayaGram ("we", "our", or "the Application"), a high-performance messaging client built on the official Telegram API protocol. Your privacy and digital security are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled when you use NayaGram on Android devices.

1 Architectural Foundation & Telegram API

NayaGram is a client application built using the open-source Telegram MTProto 2.0 protocol and Android client architecture:

  • Direct Official Connection: When you use NayaGram, your chats, media, voice calls, and account communications connect directly to Telegram official servers via encrypted MTProto sessions.
  • No Intermediary Servers: NayaGram operates zero custom proxy servers, zero data-routing backdoors, and zero intermediary log servers. All communications bypass NayaGram entirely and go straight to Telegram's cloud.
  • End-to-End Encryption: Secret Chats initiated within NayaGram utilize device-to-device end-to-end encryption. Only you and the recipient hold the decryption keys; neither NayaGram nor Telegram servers can decrypt these messages.
Please refer to the Telegram Official Privacy Policy for information on cloud storage practices governed by Telegram FZ-LLC.

2 Data We Do NOT Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles under international privacy standards:

  • No Message Interception: We do not read, store, monitor, or copy your messages, chats, contacts, or media files to any external server.
  • No Backdoor Administration: No developer, owner, or administrator account has any technical capability or backdoor power to access your private conversations, delete remote messages, or view your contacts.
  • No Third-Party Analytics Tracking: NayaGram does not embed silent background tracking frameworks, advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Facebook SDK), or invasive behavioral telemetry SDKs.

3 Data Handled Locally on Your Device

To provide messaging functionality, the application stores operational data locally on your device in secure sandbox storage:

  • Session Credentials: Your authentication token (AuthKey) generated during login is stored encrypted in Android Keystore / app-private storage.
  • Cache & Media: Downloaded images, voice notes, stickers, and documents are stored in local device cache and can be cleared at any time via Settings > Data and Storage > Storage Usage.
  • Local Preferences: App theme preferences, notification settings, and UI layout customizations are kept exclusively in local SharedPreferences.
  • Offline Power Features: Message Scheduler, Auto-Reply, and local log utilities run exclusively inside your phone's memory. No data is transmitted to us.

4 Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, NayaGram requests only standard runtime permissions strictly necessary for messaging:

  • Internet / Network Access: Required to establish encrypted MTProto communication with Telegram servers.
  • Storage / Photos & Media: Required to save downloaded files, photos, videos, and to select media files you wish to transmit.
  • Camera & Microphone: Required exclusively when taking photos, recording video messages, or participating in encrypted voice and video calls.
  • Contacts (Optional): Used locally to synchronize Telegram contacts when enabled by the user. Contacts are never uploaded to any commercial third-party marketing database.
  • Notifications: Required on Android 13+ to ensure timely notification of incoming calls and messages.

5 Developer Tools & NG Studio Security Boundaries

NayaGram contains internal developer diagnostics known as NG Studio:

  • Access to NG Studio is strictly restricted to designated developer IDs for testing local interface layouts, rendering performance, and debug logging.
  • Explicit Boundary: NG Studio has zero administrative authority over other users. It cannot view, alter, or intercept any user's personal messages, chats, or account data.

6 Account Deletion & Right to Erasure (GDPR)

You maintain complete control over your account, session, and data:

  • Instant Local Erase: Logging out or uninstalling the app deletes all local session keys and cached files immediately from your device.
  • Full Account Deletion: You can delete your entire account and associated cloud data at any time directly through Settings > Privacy and Security > Delete My Account.
  • Self-Destruct Timer: You can set an inactivity auto-delete timer (e.g., 1, 3, 6, or 12 months) under account settings.

7 Google Play Store & Regulatory Compliance

This Application strictly adheres to:

  • Google Play Developer Program Policies: Fully compliant with User Data, Permissions, and Deceptive Behavior Policies.
  • Telegram API Terms of Service: Built respecting the open-source licensing and Telegram MTProto integrity requirements.
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): Complete transparency regarding data storage, processing rights, and right to be forgotten.
  • Children's Privacy Protection: NayaGram is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors.
