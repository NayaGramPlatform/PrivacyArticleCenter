NayaGram Privacy Policy & Security Charter

Comprehensive Data Protection Protocol, Zero-Surveillance Architecture, and Compliance Standards for the NayaGram Android Messaging Client.

📅 Last Updated: October 2026 • 🔒 Protocol: Telegram MTProto 2.0 • 🏛️ Governing Entity: NayaGram Platform
Radical Commitment to Privacy: NayaGram operates on a zero-intermediary infrastructure. When you use NayaGram, your chats, voice calls, documents, and media connect directly to official Telegram cloud servers using end-to-end and transport-layer military-grade MTProto encryption. NayaGram Platform maintains zero message relay servers, zero telemetry collectors, and zero backdoors.

1 Core Architectural Principles & Direct Telegram API

NayaGram is an enhanced third-party client built strictly on the open-source Telegram MTProto client architecture:

  • Direct Server Routing: Every communication packet originates on your device and connects directly to Telegram official datacenters. NayaGram hosts NO proxy layers or middleman servers between you and the Telegram network.
  • Military-Grade MTProto Cryptography: All network traffic uses the Telegram MTProto 2.0 protocol (Diffie-Hellman 2048-bit key exchange, SHA-256, and AES-256-IGE encryption).
  • End-to-End Encrypted Secret Chats: Secret Chats are encrypted exclusively on user endpoints. The cryptographic keys reside on the sender's and receiver's devices only. Neither Telegram nor NayaGram can decrypt or inspect Secret Chats.

🔒 Official Cloud Data Protection

For cloud chat data stored within the global Telegram infrastructure, data management and retention are governed directly by Telegram FZ-LLC under the Telegram Official Privacy Policy.

2 Radical Zero-Surveillance Policy: What We Never Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization. NayaGram strictly enforces the following guarantees:

🚫 Zero Message Interception

We do not read, monitor, intercept, copy, or upload your messages, contacts, or voice recordings to any external server.

🚫 Zero Administrative Backdoors

No administrator, developer, or operator possesses technical capabilities to view private chats, force account changes, or delete remote messages.

🚫 Zero Third-Party Analytics SDKs

NayaGram contains NO Google AdMob, NO Facebook SDK, NO Firebase Analytics, and NO invasive behavioral tracking telemetry.

🚫 Zero Device Fingerprinting

We never track or monetize your Android Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, or device hardware identifiers.

3 Sandbox Local Storage & Cryptographic Security

All operational data necessary for client functionality is restricted entirely to your local device sandbox:

  • Authentication Tokens (AuthKey): Your Telegram authentication keys are generated during login and stored inside encrypted app-private sandbox storage protected by Android OS security isolation.
  • Local Cache Management: Cached images, voice notes, stickers, and documents reside locally and can be permanently cleared at any time via Settings > Data and Storage > Storage Usage.
  • Advanced Power Utilities: Features such as Message Scheduler, Auto-Reply, and local message logs execute strictly in-memory on your device without communicating with external endpoints.

4 Android Device Permissions & Legitimate Usage

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, NayaGram requests only runtime permissions required to perform standard messaging functions:

  • Internet Access: Required to establish encrypted MTProto communication with Telegram cloud servers.
  • Storage / Media: Required to download and save documents, photos, audio, and videos to your device, and to select media you choose to send.
  • Camera & Microphone: Accessed ONLY when you actively record video messages, snap photos in-app, or engage in encrypted voice/video calls.
  • Contacts (Optional): Synchronizes phone contacts locally with your Telegram account upon your explicit consent. Your phonebook is NEVER uploaded to any marketing server.
  • Notifications: Required on Android 13+ (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) to deliver instant message and call notifications.

5 NG Studio Internal Diagnostic Boundaries

NayaGram includes an internal engineering suite designated as NG Studio:

  • NG Studio is restricted strictly to designated internal developer IDs for rendering tests, layout debugging, and UI frame measurement.
  • Strict Boundary Guarantee: NG Studio has zero administrative authority over ordinary users. It possesses NO mechanism to view, intercept, or tamper with any user's personal communications, accounts, or messages.

6 Account Deletion & Right to Erasure (GDPR)

You hold unconditional ownership and control over your digital footprint:

  • Instant Device Purge: Logging out or uninstalling NayaGram immediately deletes all local cryptographic keys and cached assets from your phone.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You can permanently wipe your account and all associated cloud data at any time via Settings > Privacy and Security > Delete My Account.
  • Automated Self-Destruct: You can configure an inactivity auto-delete interval (1, 3, 6, or 12 months) under your security settings.

7 Google Play Store Compliance & Child Protection

NayaGram operates under strict compliance with global digital safety regulations:

  • Google Play Policies: Full adherence to User Data, Permissions, Financial Fraud, and Deceptive Behavior Policies.
  • Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA): NayaGram is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors.
  • Open-Source Compliance: Distributed in accordance with the GNU General Public License as an open ecosystem client.
