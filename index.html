Privacy Policy

Official privacy disclosure and data safety policy for NayaGram Android Application.

NayaGram Platform Google Play Store Compliant Effective: October 2026
Welcome to NayaGram, an enhanced high-performance messaging client powered by the official open-source Telegram MTProto protocol. This Privacy Policy details our radical commitment to your personal privacy, zero data collection, and direct encrypted communication.

1 Radical Zero Data Collection

NayaGram operates under a strict principle of data minimization and zero interception:

  • No Personal Server Logging: We do NOT host intermediary database servers that collect, store, sell, or analyze your messages, photos, voice notes, or contacts.
  • No Account Profiling: We do NOT track your behavior, active hours, or communication patterns.
  • Zero Message Interception: All transmissions occur directly between your device and Telegram's official global cloud infrastructure.

2 Official Telegram API Integration

NayaGram utilizes the official Telegram API protocol (api.telegram.org / MTProto 2.0):

🔒 Direct End-to-End & Cloud Encryption

Your login credentials, SMS confirmation codes, secret chats, and cloud chats are encrypted using MTProto military-grade cryptography directly with Telegram servers. NayaGram developers have zero ability to decrypt or read your messages. Please inspect Telegram Official Privacy Policy.

3 Android System Permissions & Purpose

NayaGram asks only for device permissions strictly necessary to deliver messaging features:

  • Storage & Photos / Media: Required to save downloaded files, photos, and voice notes to your local device, and to select media for sending.
  • Camera & Microphone: Used exclusively when you record video messages, snap photos in-app, or conduct encrypted voice/video calls.
  • Contacts (Optional): Allows local contact matching with Telegram accounts. Your contacts are NEVER copied or uploaded to any third-party marketing server.
  • Notifications: Required on Android 13+ to ensure timely notification of incoming calls and chats.

4 On-Device Only Power Features

NayaGram includes advanced user utilities designed for privacy and productivity:

  • Message Scheduler & Auto-Reply: Operates entirely offline inside your phone's local sandbox memory.
  • Anti-Delete & Message Logs: Stored locally inside the app's protected sandbox database. No external telemetry is transmitted.
  • Clear Data Anytime: All local data can be cleared instantly via Settings > Data and Storage > Clear Cache.

5 No Third-Party Telemetry or Ad SDKs

NayaGram contains ZERO advertisements and ZERO invasive commercial analytics SDKs (no Facebook SDK, no AppsFlyer, no ad networks). Your device identifier and advertising ID are never shared.

6 Account Deletion & Right to Erasure (GDPR)

You maintain 100% control over your account and personal presence:

  • Uninstall / Logout: Removes all local keys, cache files, and settings immediately from your Android device.
  • Delete Account: You can completely terminate your Telegram account and wipe all cloud history anytime through Settings > Privacy and Security > Delete My Account.

7 Child Privacy Protection

NayaGram is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors.
